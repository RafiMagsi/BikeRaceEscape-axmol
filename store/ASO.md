## Long description (both)
Dino Bike Escape: Sewer Run is a fast arcade bike racer. Jump ramps, dodge zombie sewer rats, collect coins,
unlock new bikes and outrun the dinosaurs before they catch you.

- Challenging levels through neon-lit sewers and night-time streets
- Collect coins and unlock new bikes
- Kids Bike mode for younger riders
- Remove Ads with a one-time purchase
